# PixelPage vs Unbounce: A/B Testing and Speed Compared

Both platforms promise better conversions — but they get there differently.

**Unbounce** built its reputation on optimization. Its Smart Traffic feature uses machine learning to route each visitor to whichever page variant is statistically likely to convert them, based on device, location, and behavior. Unbounce claims this delivers meaningfully higher conversions than standard 50/50 A/B testing. It's a genuinely strong feature — but it's only available on the Experiment plan and up, priced around $112/month, and pages are hosted on Unbounce's own infrastructure.

**PixelPage** approaches testing from the creation side rather than the traffic side. Its **Built-In A/B Testing** feature automatically generates Variant A and Variant B — with alternate headlines and CTA copy — the moment your page is created, so you're testing messaging from day one instead of setting up experiments manually. Combined with chat-based real-time editing, you can tweak a headline or CTA and regenerate a new variant in seconds.

**On speed:** PixelPage's core promise is producing a production-ready page in minutes from a single prompt, with lightweight, semantic HTML/CSS export built for fast load times — no page builder overhead.

**Bottom line:** Unbounce excels at optimizing traffic across existing pages long-term. PixelPage excels at getting multiple well-written, testable variants live fast — ideal for agencies and marketers who need volume without sacrificing conversion focus.
