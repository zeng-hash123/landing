# PixelPage vs Instapage: Which Is Better for Agencies Managing Multiple Clients?

Agencies don't need another landing page tool — they need fewer bottlenecks. That's where PixelPage and Instapage take different approaches.

**Instapage** is a mature, enterprise-grade builder known for its drag-and-drop editor, AdMap®, and server-side testing. But its pricing reflects that maturity: entry plans start around $99/month and lack A/B testing, which only unlocks on the pricier Optimize tier (roughly $199/month). For agencies juggling many small client budgets, that cost adds up fast, and someone still has to design and write copy for every page.

**PixelPage** takes a different route. Instead of a manual editor, three specialized AI agents — copywriter, designer, and compliance — build the page for you from a single prompt or an existing ad URL. For agencies specifically, the standout is **Client-Specific Brand Isolation**: switch between client logos, colors, and fonts instantly without cross-contaminating brand kits, then export clean, white-labeled HTML with no PixelPage branding.

**Where Instapage wins:** teams that want granular, pixel-level manual control and deep native integrations.

**Where PixelPage wins:** speed and scale. Agencies can generate and launch a client-ready page in minutes instead of managing design queues, and built-in compliance checks catch policy risks before pages hit Meta or Google Ads — something Instapage doesn't offer natively.

If your agency ships dozens of campaign variants a week across different clients, PixelPage's multi-agent workflow removes the developer and copywriter bottleneck entirely.
