# How to Create a High-Converting Landing Page

A landing page has one job: get the visitor to take a single action. Here's how to build one that actually does that.

**1. Write one clear headline.** Your headline should state the specific outcome, not a vague benefit. "Write cold emails that get 40% reply rates" converts better than "Better email marketing." Specificity builds trust instantly.

**2. Match the page to the ad.** If someone clicks an ad about a discount, they should land on a page about that discount — not your generic homepage. Message match is one of the biggest silent conversion killers.

**3. Keep the CTA visible and repeated.** Don't bury your call-to-action below three scrolls of copy. Repeat it after your hero section, after your features, and again at the bottom.

**4. Cut the copy, not the persuasion.** Every sentence should either remove a doubt or build desire. If it does neither, delete it. Short, punchy sections almost always outperform dense paragraphs.

**5. Add proof near the decision point.** Testimonials, client logos, or specific numbers ("500+ agencies") work best right before your CTA, not buried in a separate section.

**6. Check for compliance before you launch.** Vague guarantees or absolute claims can get pages flagged or rejected by Meta and Google Ads — costing you time and ad spend.

**7. Test variants, don't guess.** Even small headline or CTA changes can shift conversion rates significantly. Always launch with at least two variants running.

**8. Keep it fast.** Slow-loading pages lose visitors before they even see your offer. Lightweight, semantic HTML beats bloated page-builder code every time.
